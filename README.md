# 📦 Gaalops Menstrual Cup Wholesale Stock Management Application

A complete, easy-to-use, offline-ready stock & inventory application built specifically for **Wholesale Menstrual Cup Distribution** (IndiaMART sellers).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **15-SKU Live Stock Matrix**:
   - **3 Sizes**: Small (S), Medium (M), Large (L).
   - **5 Shapes**: Classic Bell, Round Ball, Ergonomic Angled, Flat Collapsible, Ring Stem Grip.
   - Live visual grid showing exact piece count per size & shape.
2. **Factory Stock In (Receipts)**:
   - When your manufacturing supplier sends cups, record the batch, quantity, and cost price with 1 click.
3. **Wholesale Stock Out (IndiaMART Dispatches)**:
   - When a buyer orders on IndiaMART, log the buyer's name, phone, city, quantity, and selling rate. Stock is automatically deducted!
4. **Low Stock Alerts**:
   - Notifies you when any shape or size drops below minimum safety threshold (e.g., 50 pcs), so you never run out of stock for IndiaMART leads.
5. **Stock Valuation & Profit Overview**:
   - Total cups in warehouse, total investment value at cost price, and wholesale selling realization.
6. **Wholesale Buyer Directory**:
   - Automatically maintains a directory of all your repeat IndiaMART buyers, their cities, and order volume.
7. **Excel & CSV Export**:
   - Download complete inventory or transaction ledger into Excel / CSV anytime.
8. **100% Offline & Private**:
   - All data is saved securely on your device. Works without internet connection.

---

## 🚀 How to Run on PC / Laptop (Windows)

1. Simply double-click **`Launch-App.bat`** in this folder.
2. The application will open immediately in standalone application window mode using Edge or Chrome!
3. Alternatively, double-click **`index.html`** in any browser.

---

## 📱 How to Use on Your Android Phone (Like an APK / Mobile App)

You can easily use this on your Android phone without needing an app store:

### Option A: Direct Browser / Home Screen Icon (Recommended)
1. Copy the folder `GAALOPS STOCK APPLICATION` to your phone (via USB cable or Google Drive / WhatsApp).
2. Open Chrome on your Android phone and open `index.html`.
3. Tap the **3 vertical dots menu (⋮)** in Chrome (top right).
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.
5. An icon called **"Gaalops Stock"** will appear on your phone's home screen with the app logo, opening full-screen just like a regular APK!

## 💡 How Wholesale Stock Works (Simple Guide for Beginners)

| Action | When to do this? | What happens? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stock In (Factory)** | When your manufacturing supplier delivers boxes | Stock count **increases (+)** |
| **Stock Out (IndiaMART)** | When an IndiaMART buyer places a bulk order and you dispatch | Stock count **decreases (−)** |
| **Stock Matrix** | To check which shape/size is available right now | Displays all 15 variations |
| **Export Excel** | To send stock list to buyers or your CA | Downloads a `.csv` file for Excel |

## 💾 Backing Up Your Data

Go to the **Settings & Backup** tab in the app:
- Click **"Download Complete Backup (.json)"** to save a backup copy.
- If you change computers or phones, click **"Restore Data from Backup File"** to restore all your records instantly.
