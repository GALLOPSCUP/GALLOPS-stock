📱 How to Use as Mobile App (Android / Phone)

How to turn this application into an Android mobile icon:

  1. Method 1 (Instant Home Screen App): Copy this folder to your phone or host it on free GitHub Pages / local network. Open in Chrome on your phone, tap the 3 dots (⋮) menu in Chrome, and choose "Add to Home screen" / "Install App". It installs just like an APK with its own icon!
  2. Method 2 (On your PC / Laptop): Simply double click Launch-App.bat anytime on your computer. It runs completely offline without internet!
  3. Offline Working: Your stock data is stored safely in your browser storage. You can check stock and add entries even without internet.
